The Genesis of Voltage

Crafted Where Lightning
Strikes The Kettle.

We started Bolt with a single radical thesis: craft beer had grown too comfortable. The world was flooded with dull ales and predictable lagers that lacked punch and vitality.

In our alpine micro-brewery, we pioneered the Ionized Cryo-Shock technique. By flash-freezing Pacific Northwest hops at -196°C and steeping them through pulsed electromagnetic agitation, we extract pure, unoxidized essential oils with microscopic precision.

The result is a mouthfeel so crisp, explosive, and hyper-defined, it feels like tasting liquid electricity for the very first time. No fillers. No artificial carbonation. Just raw botanical lightning.

Engineering Blueprint

Anatomy of a Strike.

Every millimeter of a Bolt vessel is engineered to preserve maximum aroma voltage and cryogenic freshness.

01

Diamond Shield™ Top Seal

Zero dissolved oxygen tolerance. Micro-crimped double seal prevents any ambient oxidation from degrading delicate hop terpenes.

02

Ionized Spring Extraction

High-altitude mineral spring water passed through magnetic ionizers to balance calcium sulfate ratios for razor-sharp crispness.

03

Sub-Zero Nitrogen Dosing

A micro-burst of liquid nitrogen dosed 0.05 seconds prior to seaming creates a velvety, dense head of electric foam.

04

Thermal UV Blackout Core

Lightproof aluminium armor prevents skunking from solar UV rays, guaranteeing fresh-from-the-tank taste anywhere in the world.

The 4-Step Voltage Circuit

How Voltage is Born.

A relentless four-stage brewing protocol calibrated to the nearest tenth of a degree and single microvolt.

Phase 01 // Input

Ionized Spring Extraction

We draw glacier run-off water and subject it to a multi-stage magnetic resonance chamber to ensure optimal hop oil solubility.

Target PPM: 120 Stage: Ready
Phase 02 // Mash

Cryo-Shock Mashing

Organic two-row barley is crushed under low-oxygen nitrogen blankets, converting starches into clean, highly fermentable sugars.

Temp: 65.2°C Stage: Active
Phase 03 // Hop Strike

Electrified Dry-Hop

Pulsed ultrasonic vibrations circulate cryo-pelletized hops through the fermentation vessel, locking in maximum tropical aroma without bitterness harshness.

Pressure: 1.8 Bar Stage: Pulsing
Phase 04 // Condition

Sub-Zero Conditioning

Lagering at -1.5°C for 28 straight days drops any remaining yeast haze, producing immaculate brilliance and a crystalline, razor-sharp clean swallow.

Temp: -1.5°C Stage: Chilling
